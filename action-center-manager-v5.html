Why these focus areas?

Focus areas are identified based on four factors from your team's survey results:

  • Trend — whether your team's score on this question has declined since the last survey
  • Gap — how your team's score compares to the rest of the organization
  • Consistency — how aligned your team members are in their responses
  • Performance — how your score compares to internal and external benchmarks

Questions that score highest across these four factors are surfaced as your top focus areas.

Why these successes?

Successes are identified based on the same four factors from your team's survey results:

  • Trend — whether your team's score on this question has improved since the last survey
  • Gap — how your team's score compares to the rest of the organization
  • Consistency — how aligned your team members are in their responses
  • Performance — how your score compares to internal and external benchmarks

Questions that score best across these four factors are surfaced as your top successes.
