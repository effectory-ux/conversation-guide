3

Guidance per step

Opening, selecting, celebrating

Use these prompts as a light reference. Adapt them to the flow of the conversation rather than reading them as a script.

One conversation, one focus area. Present all three, check what resonates, then explore one together. Covering all three in one meeting leaves nothing decided.
1

Open the conversation

Set the purpose: understand the experience behind the results before deciding what to do. Create safety by agreeing to listen to understand, making room for different experiences, and discussing patterns rather than individual responses.

Conversation starter

“These results tell us where to look, not why they are what they are. I would like us to understand the experience behind them together.”

2

Look at the overall results and select a focus area

Put the results on the screen from Focus view in My Effectory, then walk through the overall picture and the three focus areas. Check whether they resonate, and guide the team to select one area to explore further.

Open the picture

Dive deeper into the selected focus area

Choose two or three starters, listen to the responses, ask a follow up where useful, check whether others recognise the pattern, and summarise what you hear.
